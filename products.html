04How to apply

Two coats.
One smooth wall.

The same simple process for every BHRATA putty.

  1. PrepareClean the wall of dust, loose paint and grease. Dampen dry plaster lightly before cement putty.
  2. MixAdd powder to clean water and mix to a smooth, lump-free paste. Acrylic putty is ready to use.
  3. ApplySpread a thin first coat with a putty blade. Let it dry, then apply a second coat across it.
  4. FinishSand lightly with fine emery paper, wipe off dust, then prime and paint.
