Every leg of the journey

Wall putty made in Gujarat — white cement, acrylic and waterproof — and shipped to builders across Africa by one accountable team.

Scroll
01Network

Fourteen ports.
One chain.

From our plant in Morbi, Gujarat, to thirteen ports across Africa, every lane runs through a single team. We zoom in on one of them — a 40′ box of BHRATA putty on its way to Mundra port.

02Factory · Mixing

Mixed
right.

Three silos feed white cement, polymer binder and additives into the ribbon mixer — batch after batch, for the same smooth finish every time.

02Factory · Packing

Packed
tight.

The mix is filled into 20 kg BHRATA bags, sealed and carried down the line, then stacked six layers high on every pallet.

02Factory · Loading

Into
the box.

The forklift drives each pallet up the ramp into the container waiting on the truck at our loading bay. Doors shut, seal on — ready for the road.

02Road · Container yard

To the
yard.

The sealed box rolls out of the Morbi plant to the BHRATA container yard — gate-in and customs scan, then cleared for the port road.

03Road

To the
port.

The truck runs it from the factory down to Mundra, checks in at Gate 02 and drives straight to the berth. The orange line is the record, drawn live.

04Sea · Berth 3

Onto the
vessel.

The ship-to-shore crane lifts it off the truck, trolleys out over the water and stows it in bay 06.

04Sea · Open water

Across the
ocean.

Lines cast off at dusk. Same container, same record — now at fourteen knots toward Mombasa.

05Air

And over
it all.

Samples, shade cards and urgent dealer orders fly overhead — booked, cleared and tracked by the same team.
